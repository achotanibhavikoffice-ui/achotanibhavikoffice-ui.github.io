Origin & Lived Philosophy

Adversity Turned into Systematic Discipline

Grounded roots, academic perseverance, and engineering that relieves human friction.

My pathway into engineering was forged through significant personal adversity. Coming from a modest background where higher educational and financial security were never given, I learned early that sustained resilience and unyielding hard work are the only dependable equalizers in life.

Growing up around my family's community pharmacy counter in Ulhasnagar, I watched ordinary people make agonizing trade-offs between urgent medicines and monthly household survival. That experience permanently stripped away any tolerance for superficial engineering: technology is only as valuable as the real-world suffering it relieves.

Rather than letting difficulties limit my trajectory, I converted life's pressures into academic focus. I earned admission into Thadomal Shahani Engineering College (University of Mumbai), maintained a spotless 8.08 / 10.0 CGPA with zero backlogs, authored three research preprints, and engineered 5 live cloud deployments. Every project I design, whether empowering Marathi and Hindi-speaking farmers through DhanSathi or calibrating Bayesian MRI segmentation in NeuroSeg, is driven by this lived ethos: turning struggle into technology that serves.

NeuroSeg: A Bi-Hemispheric Spectral-Attention U-Net Workspace with Signed Distance Boundary Supervision, Bayesian Uncertainty Calibration, and Cross-Modal Neuro-Hematology Fusion

Bhavik Achotani (2026) • Zenodo Research Archive • IEEE Two-Column Technical Paper

Architected an end-to-end clinical neuro-oncology PACS workspace uniting a 4-stage Spectral-Transformer Attention U-Net (2D FFT high-pass gates + contralateral bi-hemispheric cross-attention) with a 6-layer MRI viewer. Formulated a GPU morphological Signed Distance Map (SDM) boundary loss and Bayesian Monte Carlo dropout engine (T=8 passes), boosting Dice Similarity from 83.10% to 91.84%, cutting HD95 error by 60.4% (3.42mm), and achieving 0.0192 ECE. Trained a Cross-Modal Gated Fusion Network on 12,000 patient records fusing 3D MRI with 12 blood biomarkers & sGFAP (94.33% 4-class differential accuracy).
